Welcome to DashBill

Budget, project and GST invoicing software that keeps its books in a database you own. Two short steps: tell it where to keep your data, then put your own business name and logo on it. This is a one-time setup — it is remembered from the next launch onwards.

Paste your connection string

the quickest way
In TiDB Cloud open your cluster, press Connect, choose General as the connect-with option, and copy the connection string it shows. Paste it here and the fields below fill themselves in.
